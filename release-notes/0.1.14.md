ASUS lights come back on their own after the PC was locked.

**Fixes**
- While the PC is locked or asleep Synapse sends no frames, and Armoury Crate takes the ASUS lights back. Aura kept accepting ChromaBridge's writes afterwards without showing them, so the board and strips stayed on Armoury Crate's effect while the window said everything was following. The ASUS host now reconnects to Aura when frames resume after a gap of 30 seconds or more, which requests control again; the ASUS lights rejoin a few seconds after the others.
