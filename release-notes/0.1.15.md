A watchdog for writes that never return.

**Fixes**
- A vendor's SDK can block forever inside a write, typically one made as its software comes back after the PC was locked. The device then got no updates, with no failure and nothing in the log, until its provider was switched off and on. Seen with iCUE. Each host now notices a frame that has waited ten seconds for the write before it, logs which device, and restarts itself; the lights rejoin a few seconds later. This covers every provider.
